Chase

Designing Chase Wealth Plan, a single advisor-led plan for retirement, education, and healthcare goals

Product design for web and mobile. Winner, Smart Money Awards Best Digital Experience.

Product design UX design
Fintech Wealth management Advisor tools

The problem

Investors underestimate retirement spending. Retirement, education, and healthcare goals were kept technically separate even when a client's real plan combined them. Clients couldn't edit their own goal details without contacting an advisor, and inputs like Social Security amount were collected during onboarding, disconnected from the goal that actually needed them.

The approach

As the product designer on Wealth Plan, I led the redesign of how goals came together, letting related goals combine into a single plan instead of staying siloed. I rebuilt goal setup so clients could manage more of their own plan, without waiting on an advisor for every change.
