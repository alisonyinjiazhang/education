Alison Zhang

Learning Experience / UX Designer


Where learning science meets product design.

I design learning experiences and digital products. My graduate study in Instructional Technology and Media at Columbia grounded my work in learning science and curriculum design. Nearly ten years of product design at Chase, Coach, and BitGo added visual hierarchy, storytelling, and front-end practice, which I use to make complex ideas clear and actionable.

I work across the full arc of a learning project, from needs analysis to launch: Articulate Storyline and Rise courses, facilitator materials, and interactive practice built around application rather than passive content. Past work includes STEM courses on Moodle and Cornerstone, science inquiry software, learning environments on AGI and cyberbullying prevention, and after-school programming at the Anna Silver School. Recent product work, such as a learning hub for Capital One, draws on the same approach: structure, visual storytelling, and interaction in service of understanding.
