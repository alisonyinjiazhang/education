Capital One

Designing Capital One's learning and development hub

A course catalog, HR and admin links, and a peer feedback flow, all in one dashboard. Product design in Figma, using the design system.

Product design UX design
L&D Fintech Dashboard

The problem

Training buried in menus. Corporate L&D tools have a low bar and usually clear it by being merely functional. Courses, HR tasks, and team resources tend to live in separate places, so getting through required training means bouncing between tools before you even reach the content.

The approach

One dashboard, not five tabs. Brought it into a single hub: a course catalog with mixed video and reading lessons, a Team Resources page, and Quick HR & Admin Reminders surfaced right on the dashboard instead of buried in a separate portal. A built-in review flow lets employees leave feedback right after finishing a course, and an in-app chat covers questions that don't fit neatly into a course page.
