Coach

Redesigning Coach's shopping experience from browse to checkout

Product design for web and mobile that keeps the brand's craft front and center while customers move from browse to checkout. Conversion up 200%.

Product design Visual design
Retail E-commerce

The problem

Craft versus checkout. Luxury e-commerce has to hold two things in tension: the tactile, editorial feel of the brand, and the practical mechanics of a fast, frictionless checkout. Lean too far toward either and you lose either the brand or the sale.

The approach

Let the product carry the brand. Built a shopping flow where product photography and editorial moments carried the brand voice, while navigation, filtering, and checkout stayed deliberately plain and fast — the craft shows up in the product, not in the friction.
